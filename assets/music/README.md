The 17 supplied MP3s live here as `track-01.mp3` through `track-17.mp3`. Their displayed titles and relative paths are listed in `music.tracks` in `content.js`. To add a song, put its MP3 here and add `{ title: "actual title", src: "assets/music/filename.mp3" }` to that array. To remove one, delete its array entry. The player never starts on page load; the visitor must press `[play]`. `[next]` selects a random track, and a finished song starts another random track. Selection slightly favors titles containing “Artificial Angels” (weight 1.3 versus 1.0) while avoiding an immediate repeat when possible.
